What is real and what is illustrative
- Real: the observed facts quoted in the narration (about 7,500 light-years, Carina region, about 5.5 light-years across,
  Hubble visible vs Webb infrared views, dust glowing in mid-infrared, possible young star at the "eye", erosion by
  radiation and winds from nearby massive stars).
- Illustrative: every 3-D shape, camera move, particle flow, the galaxy map positions (approximate, from galactic
  longitude and distance), the erosion time-lapse (no timescale is published or implied) and the light-travel
  "flight". Each such scene carries an on-screen tag saying so.
